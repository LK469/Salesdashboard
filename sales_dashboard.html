⚙ Partner Setup Guide
Two ways to tag partner-sourced opportunities

Option A — Salesforce field (recommended)
Add Partner_Account__c (or similar) to your Opportunity object in Salesforce, then add it to your Coefficient SOQL: SELECT …, Partner_Account__c FROM Opportunity The dashboard picks it up automatically on next refresh.

Option B — Manual overlay tab (no SF changes needed)
In the same Google Sheet as your Coefficient data, create a tab named Partners with three columns:

OppId Partner Partner Type
006Ps00000…OktaReferral
006Ps00000…CrowdStrikeCo-Sell

Copy the Opportunity Id from Salesforce (or from the Opportunity tab in the sheet). Hit ↻ Refresh from Sheets after each update — no redeployment needed.
